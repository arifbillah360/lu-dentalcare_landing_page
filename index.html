Frequently Asked Questions

Find answers to the most common questions about dental implants, procedures, costs, and what to expect during your treatment.

What are dental implants and how do they work?
+

Dental implants are titanium posts that are surgically placed into your jawbone to act as artificial tooth roots. They fuse with your bone through a process called osseointegration, providing a stable foundation for replacement teeth. Once healed, a custom crown, bridge, or denture is attached to the implant, giving you a natural-looking and fully functional replacement tooth that can last a lifetime with proper care.

+

The total cost depends on factors like the number of implants needed, whether bone grafting is required, and the type of restoration. We offer free consultations where we'll provide a personalized treatment plan and exact pricing based on your specific needs.

Does the dental implant procedure hurt?
+

Most patients report minimal discomfort during and after the procedure. We use local anesthesia during surgery, and we're certified in conscious sedation for anxious patients. After the procedure, any discomfort can typically be managed with over-the-counter pain medication. Most patients return to normal activities within 24-48 hours and describe the recovery as easier than expected.

How long do dental implants last?
+

With proper care and maintenance, dental implants can last a lifetime. The implant itself (the titanium post) has a success rate of over 95% and rarely needs replacement. The crown or restoration on top may need replacement after 10-15 years due to normal wear. We provide a 5-year clinical guarantee and include 2 hygiene appointments to help ensure the longevity of your implants.

Am I a candidate for dental implants?
+

Most people with missing teeth are candidates for dental implants. Ideal candidates have adequate bone density, healthy gums, and good overall health. Even if you have bone loss, we can often perform bone grafting to make implants possible. The best way to know if you're a candidate is through a free consultation which includes a CBCT scan to evaluate your bone structure and create a personalized treatment plan.

What is the dental implant process timeline?
+

The complete process typically takes 3-6 months. First, the implant is surgically placed and needs 2-4 months to fuse with the bone. During healing, you may have a temporary tooth. Once healed, an abutment is attached, and impressions are taken for your custom crown. The final crown is placed 2-3 weeks later. We also offer same-day implants where you can receive a temporary tooth immediately after implant placement.

What's the difference between dental implants and dentures?
+

Traditional dentures rest on your gums and can slip, click, or require messy adhesives. Dental implants are permanently anchored in your jawbone, providing superior stability and restoring up to 98% of chewing ability. Implants also prevent bone loss that occurs with dentures, maintain facial structure, and feel completely natural. While implants have a higher upfront cost, they're more comfortable, functional, and cost-effective long-term.
